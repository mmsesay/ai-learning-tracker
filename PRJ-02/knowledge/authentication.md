# LEPA — Authentication & Login

## Overview

Teachers and administrators sign in to LEPA with a school email and password. Sessions expire after inactivity.

## Common tasks

### Log in
1. Open the LEPA portal URL provided by your school.
2. Enter your school email and password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

### Forgot password
1. On the login screen, click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your school email.
3. Check email for a reset link (valid for 60 minutes).
4. Set a new password (at least 8 characters).

### Account locked
After 5 failed attempts, LEPA locks the account for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again, or ask an admin to unlock you under **Admin → Users**.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to try |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| “Invalid credentials” | Wrong email/password | Reset password; confirm you use the school email, not a personal Gmail |
| Blank page after login | Browser cache / blocked cookies | Use a private window; allow cookies for the LEPA domain |
| OTP / 2FA not arriving | SMS delay or wrong phone on file | Wait 2 minutes; ask admin to verify phone under your user profile |
| “Session expired” mid-work | Idle timeout | Save work frequently; sign in again |

## Who can help
- Password resets for yourself: self-service **Forgot password**
- Unlock / role changes: school **LEPA administrator**
